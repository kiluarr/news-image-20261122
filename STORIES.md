# The 38 stories of Sunday 22 November 2026

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Sunday 22 November
2026. Each was checked against the One Turn day page for 2026-11-22
(https://oneturn.news/day/2026-11-22/), which lists the story with its
sources, and against the publication named. A few events happened late on
Saturday 21 November (the cartel capture, the Mercury insertion, the
factory fire, and the NWSL final) and were first reported in full on
Sunday 22 November;
they are marked *(Sat event, first reported Sun)*. Nothing here is from
any other day.

## Headlines (30)

1. **COP31 collapses without a deal** — The UN climate summit in Antalya,
   Turkey, ended Sunday with no agreement, two days past its deadline,
   after the EU, the UK, Panama and climate-vulnerable states rejected
   the final draft because it did not mention fossil fuels.
   Source: The Guardian, 22 Nov 2026.
2. **Russian drone hits apartment block near Kyiv; 14 missing** — A drone
   struck a five-story building in Dymer, north of Kyiv, on Sunday. Two
   people were killed and at least 14 are missing under the rubble.
   Source: Euronews / Le Monde, 22 Nov 2026.
3. **Iran strikes Bahrain site; Gulf states condemn attack** — Bahrain said
   Iranian projectiles hit an industrial site in Sitra, injuring two
   people. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE condemned the attack; two
   tankers were also hit in the Strait of Hormuz.
   Source: France 24 / Japan Times, 22 Nov 2026.
4. **Global stocks fall as AI sell-off deepens** — US stocks fell into a
   correction, with the S&P 500 down 3.7% on the day; Nvidia had its
   longest weekly losing streak since 2008.
   Source: CNBC, 22 Nov 2026.
5. **Drone-hit Russian ship burns in Gulf of Finland** — The sanctioned
   cargo ship Sparta II was still burning a day after a drone attack;
   separately an explosion hit the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi
   port in Libya. Source: BBC News, 22 Nov 2026.
6. **Settlers torch vehicles in West Bank villages; Israel opens inquiry**
   — Israeli settlers set fire to 12 Palestinian vehicles and sprayed
   racist graffiti in four villages near Ramallah overnight; Israel

7. **Eisenkot invites Netanyahu's Likud to coalition talks** — Gadi
